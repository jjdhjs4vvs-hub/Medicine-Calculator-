💊 Medicine Dosage Calculator

Dose from weight

Total mg = weight × mg/kg; volume = total mg ÷ mg/mL.

Tablet or liquid dose

Amount = ordered dose ÷ strength.

IV infusion rate

mL/hr = volume ÷ hours; drops/min = (volume × drops/mL) ÷ (hours × 60).

For study/practice only. Always verify with a prescriber, protocol, and pharmacy.
